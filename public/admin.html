Teach the AI Coach your way of doing things

Everything here becomes the Coach's main source. It follows your verified answers first, then your videos and documents, then the playbook. Add your YouTube video transcripts, course docs, supplier lists, pricing rules, anything you'd tell a student.

Getting a video's transcript (any video)
  1. Open the video on YouTube.
  2. Under the title, click …more, then Show transcript.
  3. Click inside the transcript panel, select all the text and copy it.
  4. Paste it below and add the video's title and link. Timestamps are removed for you.
Many videos at once (your own channel)
  1. Open YouTube Studio → Subtitles and pick a video.
  2. Next to the language, open the ⋮ menu and choose Download (.srt or .vtt).
  3. Repeat for each video, then drag all the files into the box below. Each file name becomes the title.
  4. If a video only has automatic captions and no download option, use the copy method instead.
