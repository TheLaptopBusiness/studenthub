Words you'll hear a lot
Lot
A group of laptops sold together in one auction.
Buyer's premium
An extra fee the auction site adds on top of your winning bid, usually 10–15%.
Sold comps
What the same laptop actually sold for recently. On eBay, search the model and turn on the "Sold items" filter.
ROI (return on investment)
Your profit compared to the money you put in. 40% ROI means $40 profit for every $100 spent.
Break-even price
The lowest price each laptop can sell for without losing money.
Grade A / B / C
How good a laptop looks and works. A is like new, C shows heavy wear. See the Playbook.
BIOS lock / MDM lock
A password or company lock that stops you using the laptop. Avoid these unless the seller removes them.
Duds
Laptops in a lot that don't work. You can still sell them for parts.
